About this Event

Gather Sounds is designed for audiences who enjoy discovering contemporary Australian music in an energetic festival setting.

This event page allows users to view detailed festival information, check ticket availability, and make bookings.

Acknowledgement of Country

FestivalHub acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the land on which this event takes place, the Kaurna People of the Adelaide Plains, and pays respect to Elders past and present.

An Acknowledgement of Country recognises the Traditional Owners and continuing connection First Nations peoples have to land, waters, and culture throughout Australia.

Enhanced Acknowledgement

Gather Sounds is committed to creating a culturally respectful and inclusive festival environment. Event organisers encourage visitors to learn about the history and significance of Kaurna Country and support reconciliation through meaningful recognition, cultural awareness, and respectful engagement.
