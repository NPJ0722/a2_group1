Acknowledgement of Country

FestivalHub encourages organisers to respectfully recognise the Traditional Custodians of the land where events are held. Different acknowledgement approaches may be used depending on the scale and cultural context of the event.

Guidance

An Acknowledgement of Country can be delivered by anyone to recognise Traditional Custodians and their continuing connection to Country.

A Welcome to Country is different and should only be delivered by Traditional Owners or recognised Elders of that Country.

Creating a Meaningful Acknowledgement
  • Research the correct Traditional Custodians for the event location.
  • Avoid generic or tokenistic wording.
  • Recognise ongoing connection to land, waters, culture, and community.
  • Use respectful wording suitable for the audience and event context.
  • Generic acknowledgement: suitable for general events when the organiser wants a respectful but simple statement.
  • Enhanced acknowledgement: should include the specific Traditional Custodians, location, and a more thoughtful connection to Country.

This acknowledgement will be displayed on the Event Details page so visitors can recognise the Traditional Custodians connected to the event.
